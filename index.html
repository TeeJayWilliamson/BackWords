BackWords

Say something. Hear it flipped. Copy what you heard, flip that back, and see how close you get.

1. Say it

A short phrase, up to 8 seconds.

2. Hear it backwards

Listen a few times. Now try to say exactly what you hear.

3. Say what you heard

Copy the backwards sound as closely as you can.

4. Flip yours back

If you nailed it, this sounds like the original.
